# Neuromorphic Computing Paper Sourcing Guide

## Domain Description
Neuromorphic Computing: SNNs, neuromorphic chips, event-based sensors, spike-timing plasticity

## Recommended Search Terms
- "spiking neural network" AND "hardware"
- "neuromorphic chip" AND "benchmark"
- "event-based vision" AND "accuracy"
- "Intel Loihi" OR "IBM TrueNorth" AND "performance"
- "STDP" AND "learning"
- "DVS" OR "dynamic vision sensor"
- "spike timing dependent plasticity"

## Recommended Sources (in priority order)
1. **arXiv cs.NE**: https://arxiv.org/list/cs.NE/recent (Neural and Evolutionary Computing)
2. **Frontiers in Neuroscience**: https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/neuroscience
3. **IEEE Transactions on Neural Networks** (open articles)
4. **Nature Communications** (open access articles)

## Quality Indicators
- Papers with benchmark comparisons (accuracy tables)
- Hardware implementation results (latency, power consumption)
- Comparisons to traditional DNNs
- Quantitative claims about energy efficiency (e.g., "10x less power")
- Statistical validation (p-values, confidence intervals)

## Papers to Avoid
- Pure theory without experimental validation
- Workshop papers without peer review
- Papers behind paywall
- Opinion pieces or editorials
