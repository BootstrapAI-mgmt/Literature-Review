# Fusion Energy Paper Sourcing Guide

## Domain Description
Fusion Energy: Tokamak physics, plasma confinement, ITER, inertial confinement, stellarators

## Recommended Search Terms
- "tokamak" AND "confinement time"
- "ITER" AND "plasma performance"
- "stellarator" AND "optimization"
- "inertial confinement fusion" AND "ignition"
- "plasma instability" AND "mitigation"
- "divertor" AND "heat flux"
- "fusion reactor" AND "Q factor"

## Recommended Sources (in priority order)
1. **arXiv physics.plasm-ph**: https://arxiv.org/list/physics.plasm-ph/recent (Plasma Physics)
2. **Nuclear Fusion** (IOP Open articles)
3. **DOE OSTI**: https://www.osti.gov/ (Office of Scientific and Technical Information)
4. **Physics of Plasmas** (open articles)

## Quality Indicators
- Confinement time measurements (τE)
- Fusion power gain (Q) values
- Temperature and density profiles with uncertainties
- Comparison to scaling laws (IPB98, etc.)
- Machine performance metrics (EAST, JET, W7-X data)

## Papers to Avoid
- Purely theoretical without experimental validation
- Conference proceedings without peer review
- Outdated data (pre-2015 unless foundational)
- Marketing materials from fusion companies
