# Materials Science Paper Sourcing Guide

## Domain Description
Materials Science: Battery materials, superconductors, metamaterials, 2D materials, alloys

## Recommended Search Terms
- "lithium-ion battery" AND "cathode"
- "superconductor" AND "critical temperature"
- "metamaterial" AND "properties"
- "graphene" OR "2D materials" AND "properties"
- "high-entropy alloy" AND "mechanical properties"
- "perovskite" AND "efficiency"
- "solid-state battery" AND "conductivity"

## Recommended Sources (in priority order)
1. **arXiv cond-mat.mtrl-sci**: https://arxiv.org/list/cond-mat.mtrl-sci/recent (Materials Science)
2. **npj Computational Materials**: https://www.nature.com/npjcompumats/ (open access)
3. **Materials Today** (Elsevier open articles)
4. **ACS Energy Letters** (open articles)
5. **Nature Communications** (materials articles)

## Quality Indicators
- Energy density measurements (Wh/kg, Wh/L)
- Critical temperature (Tc) for superconductors
- Mechanical property data (tensile strength, hardness)
- Cycle life and capacity retention for batteries
- Material characterization (XRD, TEM, etc.)

## Papers to Avoid
- Claims without experimental validation
- Room-temperature superconductor claims without replication
- Papers with incomplete characterization
- Paywall-restricted content
