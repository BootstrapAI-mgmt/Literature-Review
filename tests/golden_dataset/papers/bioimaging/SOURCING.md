# Biomedical Imaging Paper Sourcing Guide

## Domain Description
Biomedical Imaging: MRI, CT, PET, ultrasound, image reconstruction, deep learning for imaging

## Recommended Search Terms
- "MRI reconstruction" AND "deep learning"
- "CT imaging" AND "dose reduction"
- "PET imaging" AND "quantification"
- "ultrasound" AND "segmentation"
- "compressed sensing" AND "medical imaging"
- "image reconstruction" AND "neural network"
- "radiomics" AND "feature extraction"

## Recommended Sources (in priority order)
1. **arXiv eess.IV**: https://arxiv.org/list/eess.IV/recent (Image and Video Processing)
2. **PubMed Central**: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/
3. **Frontiers in Radiology**: https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/radiology
4. **Scientific Reports** (imaging articles)
5. **Medical Image Analysis** (open articles)

## Quality Indicators
- Image quality metrics (SSIM, PSNR, SNR)
- Segmentation accuracy (Dice score, IoU)
- Classification performance (AUC, sensitivity, specificity)
- Reconstruction time and computational requirements
- Clinical validation with expert radiologists

## Papers to Avoid
- Studies without proper validation datasets
- Small sample sizes without cross-validation
- Papers without comparison to baseline methods
- Paywall-restricted content
