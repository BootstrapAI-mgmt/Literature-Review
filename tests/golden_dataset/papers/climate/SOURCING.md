# Climate Science Paper Sourcing Guide

## Domain Description
Climate Science: Climate models, carbon cycle, sea level rise, extreme weather, mitigation

## Recommended Search Terms
- "climate model" AND "projection"
- "sea level rise" AND "rate"
- "carbon budget" AND "emissions"
- "extreme weather" AND "attribution"
- "global warming" AND "temperature"
- "CMIP6" AND "scenario"
- "ice sheet" AND "contribution"

## Recommended Sources (in priority order)
1. **arXiv physics.ao-ph**: https://arxiv.org/list/physics.ao-ph/recent (Atmospheric and Oceanic Physics)
2. **Earth System Science Data**: https://www.earth-system-science-data.net/ (Copernicus)
3. **Nature Climate Change** (open articles)
4. **Atmospheric Chemistry and Physics**: Copernicus open access
5. **Journal of Climate** (open articles)

## Quality Indicators
- Temperature projections with uncertainty ranges
- Sea level rise rates (mm/year) with confidence intervals
- Carbon flux estimates (GtC/year)
- Model validation against observations
- Multi-model ensemble statistics

## Papers to Avoid
- Opinion pieces or policy documents
- Studies without model validation
- Single-model studies without ensemble context
- Non-peer-reviewed blogs or websites
