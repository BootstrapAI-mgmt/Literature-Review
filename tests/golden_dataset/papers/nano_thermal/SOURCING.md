# Nanoparticle Heat Transfer Paper Sourcing Guide

## Domain Description
Nanoparticle Heat Transfer: Nanofluids, thermal conductivity, heat exchangers, nanoparticle synthesis

## Recommended Search Terms
- "nanofluid" AND "thermal conductivity"
- "nanoparticle" AND "heat transfer"
- "nanoscale thermal transport"
- "Al2O3 nanofluid" OR "CuO nanofluid" AND "enhancement"
- "nanoparticle suspension" AND "convection"
- "thermal diffusivity" AND "nanoparticle"

## Recommended Sources (in priority order)
1. **arXiv cond-mat.mes-hall**: https://arxiv.org/list/cond-mat.mes-hall/recent (Mesoscale and Nanoscale Physics)
2. **Nanoscale Research Letters**: SpringerOpen
3. **International Journal of Heat and Mass Transfer** (open articles)
4. **Applied Thermal Engineering** (open articles)

## Quality Indicators
- Thermal conductivity enhancement measurements (% improvement)
- Heat transfer coefficient data with uncertainties
- Nanoparticle concentration effects (volume fraction studies)
- Comparison to base fluids with statistical analysis
- Experimental validation of theoretical models

## Papers to Avoid
- Pure simulation without experimental validation
- Studies with unclear nanoparticle characterization
- Papers without proper uncertainty analysis
- Paywall-restricted content
