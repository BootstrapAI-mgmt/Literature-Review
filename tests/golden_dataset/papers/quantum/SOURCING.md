# Quantum Computing Paper Sourcing Guide

## Domain Description
Quantum Computing: Qubit technologies, quantum algorithms, error correction, quantum supremacy

## Recommended Search Terms
- "quantum supremacy" OR "quantum advantage"
- "quantum error correction" AND "threshold"
- "superconducting qubit" AND "coherence"
- "variational quantum" AND "eigensolver"
- "quantum gate" AND "fidelity"
- "NISQ" AND "algorithm"
- "trapped ion" AND "quantum"

## Recommended Sources (in priority order)
1. **arXiv quant-ph**: https://arxiv.org/list/quant-ph/recent (Quantum Physics)
2. **PRX Quantum**: Open access
3. **Nature Communications** (quantum articles)
4. **Physical Review Letters** (open articles)

## Quality Indicators
- Gate fidelity measurements (>99% threshold)
- Coherence time data (T1, T2)
- Quantum volume or circuit depth benchmarks
- Error rates with uncertainty estimates
- Comparison to classical algorithms (speedup claims)

## Papers to Avoid
- Pure theory without hardware implementation
- Overclaiming without sufficient evidence
- Non-peer-reviewed preprints on controversial claims
- Paywall-restricted content
