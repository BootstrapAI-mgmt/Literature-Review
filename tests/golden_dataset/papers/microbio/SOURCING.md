# Microbiology & Genomics Paper Sourcing Guide

## Domain Description
Microbiology & Genomics: CRISPR, gene expression, microbiome, antibiotic resistance, sequencing

## Recommended Search Terms
- "CRISPR-Cas9" AND "efficiency"
- "microbiome" AND "diversity"
- "antibiotic resistance" AND "mechanism"
- "single-cell RNA sequencing" AND "analysis"
- "gene expression" AND "regulation"
- "metagenomics" AND "abundance"
- "genome editing" AND "off-target"

## Recommended Sources (in priority order)
1. **PubMed Central (PMC)**: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/
2. **eLife**: https://elifesciences.org/
3. **PLOS Genetics**: https://journals.plos.org/plosgenetics/
4. **PLOS Biology**: https://journals.plos.org/plosbiology/
5. **Nature Communications** (open articles)

## Quality Indicators
- Editing efficiency percentages with replicates
- Statistical analysis of gene expression changes (p-values, fold changes)
- Microbiome diversity indices (Shannon, Simpson)
- Sequencing depth and coverage statistics
- Validation experiments (qPCR confirmation, etc.)

## Papers to Avoid
- Case studies without controls
- Small sample sizes (n<3) without justification
- Papers without clear methodology sections
- Paywall-restricted content
